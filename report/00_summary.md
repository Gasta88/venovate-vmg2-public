# Summary

VMG2 proposes VENOVATE, a service platform that supports, promotes and
manages six packages developed by network experts — a broker and aggregator
across the venom-to-therapy research chain, not a performer of the work.
The finding the whole report converges on: the platform's
own revenue — VenoMate profile fees plus the 3–5% referral on work routed to
any package — is a band of {{platform.revenue.p10}}–{{platform.revenue.p90}}
a year, and every package's delivery ceiling is small against the market it
sits in.

## How to read this report

Seven parts: this Summary, then Customer, Market, Industry and Competitive,
followed by two annexes, Methods and the Validation plan. Monetary figures
are generated, never typed: they appear in the Market section and here from
one computed source. Other sections stay qualitative and point there. Claims
cite their sources; open gaps are listed, not papered over, at the end of
each section. Figures are embedded once, in one owner section, and referred
to by number elsewhere. Where a single number cannot yet be defended, the
direction is stated and the direction is labelled as such.

**Confidence markers.** Revenue bands and delivery ceilings carry inline
labels: **[LOW CONFIDENCE]** for analyst estimates not yet validated,
**[MED CONFIDENCE]** for estimates with some corroboration,
**[HIGH CONFIDENCE]** for validated numbers (rare). The Validation Plan
(Annex 2) provides the mechanism to narrow these bands through structured
interviews. Until then, treat all marked numbers as provisional.

## Proposal

- Six approved packages: P1 VENOSPATIAL (standardized spatial multi-omics
  pipeline), P2 In Silico Workshop (AlphaFold and molecular dynamics for
  venom mining), P3 Marketplace for Venom and Peptide Expertise, P4 Peptide
  Chemistry Service for disulfide-rich peptides, P5 VenoMate (expertise and
  resource matching), P6 Galleria Envenoming Model (G.E.M.) screening
  service.
- Six segments: academic labs, pharma and biotech, the AI-bio frontier,
  investors and technology-transfer offices, early-career researchers, and
  future spin-offs. Investors and TTOs are a paying segment: P3 lists due
  diligence for them.
- Three packages carry deep sizing (P3, P4, P5), three light (P1, P2, P6);
  the split governs how much evidence each sizing rests on.
- Segments are ranked by a weighted matrix of 1–5 qualitative scores; the
  Customer section owns the ranking and its rationale.

## Competitive findings

Stated categorically; the Competitive section owns the named evidence.

- No incumbent bundles the expert-discovery layer with the workflow and
  tooling layer for this field; generic players cover individual slices.
- The COST framework gives the volunteer network an institutional credibility
individual consultancies cannot claim; the VMG grant runs to October 2026
(grant letter, ref. E-COST-GRANT-IG19144-289974d6 [@grantletter]), as the
package lead confirms.
- Regulatory navigation (access and benefit-sharing under the Nagoya
  Protocol) is a sourcing-side competence competitors do not default to;
  it is not an entry barrier protecting the broker, matching or training
  lines [@nagoya].
- P5 VenoMate feeds P3 Marketplace: self-service discovery escalates into
  brokered work. That is a funnel, not cannibalisation. P3 and P4 do
  conflict: both list synthesis, folding and purification. The routing rule:
  disulfide-rich peptide requests go to P4 first; P3 brokers only what P4
  cannot do.
- Uniqueness claims get one verdict each in the Competitive section: holds,
  holds only if narrowed, or fails.

## Open risks

1. **Small ceilings, weak capacities.** Every delivery ceiling rests on an
   analyst capacity (V1–V6 in the Validation plan); none is field-tested,
   and none has been confirmed by the package leads. Lead confirmation of
   the capacities is the first post-grant step.
2. **Pricing above the observed ceiling.** P1's academic seat price exceeds
   the €150–550 ceiling in the course comparables; V1 tests it.
3. **Grant horizon.** The COST grant ends October 2026 [@grantletter];
   package revenue accrues at each lead's institution, and only P5 has
   platform-level streams — which also accrue to the host institution.
   Whether the coordinating platform survives without its own revenue base
   is a VMG4 business-model question, flagged, not resolved.
4. **Evidence depth is uneven.** The training market has pricing comparables
   but no volume basis; the investor/TTO diligence route is newly scored and
   rests on the same rule as the rest of the matrix (Customer section).
5. **Validation is a plan, not results.** Eight viability assumptions are
   scheduled for interviews; nothing ships as a result before the report date.
